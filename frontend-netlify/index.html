    How it works

    Paste a link and choose your options. The server downloads the video, transcribes the speech with Whisper, scores the transcript to find the strongest moments, and renders each clip with FFmpeg.

    Long videos take a while: expect a few minutes for a 10-minute video on a small server. Clips are deleted automatically.
